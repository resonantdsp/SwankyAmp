# Tone-stack refit

Generated by `just refit`; `just refit-check` proves it is current.

Version 2 discretises the tone stack with the standard bilinear constant
`2·SR`. Swanky Amp 1.4.0 used `SR`, which voiced every tone-stack
feature an octave above the circuit. The factory presets were voiced on
that octave-high stack, so they are refitted to sound roughly as they
did, not to replicate it: an exact match drives Low, Mid and High to
their limits.

## Method

- Input: a Karplus-Strong pluck generated in code from seed `0x24507a11`, one 0.5 s note at each of MIDI notes [40, 45, 50, 55, 59, 64, 71, 76], peaking at the level of the reference single-coil DI.
- Each preset is rendered through the shipping path at 48000 Hz with Auto oversampling, once with the released mapping (the reference) and once with the standard mapping.
- Measurements use 48 points log-spaced from 80 Hz to 8 kHz. Shape is the band levels in dB about their mean; level is the total level. The error is the mean-squared shape difference plus the squared level difference, so one dB of level counts as one dB of shape. Moving Low, Mid or High from the preset's setting adds 2 dB² per control range squared, so a control moves only as far as the match improves: without it most presets end with Mid near its maximum.
- Low, Mid and High are fitted on the tone-stack seam over a coarse 0.25 grid and two refinements to 0.01. The stack is linear and nothing before it depends on its controls, so the seam for any setting is predicted from the reference render and the ratio of the two stack responses in slices of about 1/28 octave.
- Power Drive moves only when the fitted stack still changes the level into the power stage by more than 0.5 dB, by bisection on its gain curve and never by more than 0.15 of its range.
- Every fitted preset is then rendered through the whole amplifier; the table reports those renders against the reference.

Controls are Low / Mid / High / Power Drive. Residuals are shape dB RMS
/ level dB against the released mapping. Drive is the level change into
the power stage: the seam level plus Power Drive's gain change.

## Summary

- Worst seam shape residual: 2.68 dB RMS (clean).
- Worst drive change into the power stage: 0.42 dB (distort).
- Worst output shape residual: 2.66 dB RMS (clean).
- Worst output level change: 0.67 dB (full drive).
- Presets with a control at a limit: none.

The Swanky Amp 1.4.0 build stays installable beside version 2 for
anyone who wants the original voicing exactly.
